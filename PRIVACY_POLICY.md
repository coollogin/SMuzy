# Privacy Policy for SMuzy

**Last updated:** 2026-10-15

**SMuzy** is a free and open-source software (FOSS) application distributed via GitHub. This Privacy Policy clarifies our data handling practices and how platform-level services interact with your device.

---

## 1. Summary (TL;DR)
* **Zero App Data Collection:** SMuzy does not collect, track, store, or share any personal data.
* **No Ads, Analytics, or Tracking SDKs:** There are no third-party trackers, ad networks, or analytics libraries built into the app binary.
* **Local-Only Processing:** All user input, files, and settings stay strictly on your local device.
* **Open Source:** You can inspect and verify the app's source code at [Link to Source Code Repository].

---

## 2. Information Handled by the App

### Personal Data & Identifiers
SMuzy **does not request, access, or record** personal details such as your name, email, location, contacts, or hardware IDs.

### Device Permissions
If SMuzy requests Android permissions (e.g., *Storage*, *Camera*, *Notifications*), they are used exclusively on-device to enable specific user-requested features. Permission access remains local and is never transmitted to outside servers.

---

## 3. Platform & System-Level Services (Google Play)

While SMuzy itself collects no information, downloading or updating the app via the **Google Play Store** or running it on an Android device with **Google Play Services** involves platform-level data handling managed directly by Google:

* **Google Play Store Telemetry:** Google independently collects store interaction metrics, download numbers, and OS-level crash reports under the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
* **Google Play Services:** Android system components may process anonymized device telemetry to ensure app stability and delivery.
* **App Data Safety Declaration:** In compliance with Google Play Developer Policies, SMuzy is declared in the Google Play Console as collecting **"No user data"**.

---

## 4. Network Connections & Third-Party APIs

* SMuzy allows users to connect to servers owned by them, service to which they connect voluntarily and for which they are responsible of the data exchange as standard IP addresses or standard HTTP requests. SMuzy does not offer servers for users to connect to, but users may choose to connect to third-party servers not under they control and for which they must agree independently on their privacy policies.

* SMuzy connects to the internet strictly to fetch public radio listings (https://www.radio-browser.info) if you chose local radio usage (not required if your private server handles it). Standard IP addresses are visible to the receiving server during standard HTTP requests, but no account tokens, device IDs, or tracking headers are sent.
